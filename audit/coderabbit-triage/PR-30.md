- **Location:** `contracts/facets/ConditionManagerFacet.sol:137-144` (`_enforceOracleFresh`); the validators it refers to are `contracts/libraries/LibConditionMetadata.sol:100-105` and `:211-213` (thread `PRRT_kwDOO-7Gas6uFIAW`)

  - Both creation rules compare against that lagging height, so a market maker can create a market whose answer is already public.
  - CR asks for the review's `LAG_MARGIN` on both checks, or for a staleness limit low enough to stand in for it.
- **Verification:**
  - **The review asked for a margin.** `oracle-scsvs-v10-review.md` ORC-006 recommends `(currentBlockHeight + 1 + LAG_MARGIN) / 2016 >= T / 2016` and `startBlockHeight > currentBlockHeight + LAG_MARGIN` (`:228`, `:231`, §3 `:301`).
  - **The implementation has none.** `8f5b6bf` shipped the period rule without a margin (`LibConditionMetadata.sol:102`), kept MiningDuration at `startBlockHeight <= currentBlockHeight` (`:211`), and added the staleness gate. Nothing in the commit message, `findings-ledger.md` or `follow-ups.md` records dropping the margin. The ledger row reads "fixed-clean (limit to be set on mainnet)". The PoCs anticipated a margin (`061b1a9`: "MiningDuration questions start 10 blocks past the tip (lag margin)"; ORC-003 `START_OFFSET = 10`).
  - **How large the residual is.** Lag in blocks while the oracle still passes the gate is ≈ Poisson(staleness / 600), with tip age measured from the Bitcoin header timestamp.
    - At 10800 s: mean 18; P(lag > 24) = 6.8%; P(lag > 30) = 0.33%.
    - At 3600 s: mean 6; P(lag > 10) = 4.3%; P(lag > 15) = 0.05%.
    - A tip timestamp up to 2 h ahead (allowed by ORC-010) stretches the window further.
  - **Who can exploit it.**
    - Creation is gated to the market-maker role (`LibAccessControl.enforceIsMarketMaker`). The rehearsal's market maker created 22 of the mainnet markets.
    - Exploitation needs either a market maker creating a near-term market during an oracle stall (honest or not) plus any informed taker, or a malicious market maker.
    - **Difficulty markets:** exposure only in the last `lag` blocks of a period, when the next difficulty is forecastable to about 1% anyway.
    - **MiningDuration markets:** a market starting within `lag` blocks of the oracle tip can be fully decided at creation (`blockCount ≤ lag`).
    - The 6 live mainnet markets target heights 5,500+ blocks ahead, and no MiningDuration market has ever existed on mainnet or Sepolia.
  - **The larger exposure is untouched.** Every market stays tradable on-chain for at least `SETTLEMENT_DELAY` (6 blocks) after its answer is public; `_validateOrder` gates only resolved or cancelled conditions. Neither `LAG_MARGIN` nor staleness closes that window. Only a trading cutoff does:
    - off-chain: review §8 lists it as "could not determine";
    - on-chain: needs a new EIP-7201 namespace plus a `SettlementFacet` change.
    - Neither is filed in `audit/follow-ups.md`.
  - **The margin is not free to add now.** The ORC-001 end-to-end proof uses real Feb-2010 headers 42312–42350 with tip 42328 and target 42336. That data only admits a margin ≤ 6 (`42329 + M < 42336`). ORC-003 (`START_OFFSET = 10`) and ORC-009 (`tip = B − 11`) admit ≤ 9. Larger margins mean extending `test/data/orc001_real_pow_2010/` with earlier real headers and re-anchoring the PoC. The tip+1 creations would also need new start heights:

    - `test/integration/OracleAutoResolution.test.js:156`
